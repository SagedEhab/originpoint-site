A few things
worth knowing.
Do we need everything at once?
No. We start from your current stage and build in the order your business actually needs it.
Can we start with one service?
Yes. A website, an agent, or a single integration can be the starting point.
What happens after launch?
Hosting, maintenance, and improvements are part of our approach. The scope is agreed around what you need.
What should we bring to a first conversation?
Your idea or existing website, the main problem you want to solve, and what a useful outcome would look like.
